# 01 — Problem Statement

## SIH26090

### AI-Driven Market Linkage and Smart Cataloging Mobile Application for Marginalized Artisans

## 1. Background

Traditional artisans possess valuable skills and cultural knowledge, but many of them continue to depend on local markets, intermediaries and limited personal networks to sell their products.

Moving from a traditional selling process to digital commerce requires activities such as creating product listings, preparing images, writing descriptions, deciding prices, finding suitable buyers and managing orders.

For artisans with limited digital experience, these activities can become a significant barrier to accessing wider markets.



## 2. Identified Problems

The project focuses on the following practical difficulties:

### Digital Product Creation

Artisans may find it difficult to convert a physical handmade product into a structured digital product listing.

### Manual Data Entry

Creating product descriptions and entering product information manually can be time-consuming and difficult for users who are not comfortable with digital applications.

### Language and Communication Barriers

Typing and communicating product information in a language commonly used by digital platforms can create additional difficulties.

### Pricing

The price of a handmade product can depend on several factors including material, labour, product characteristics and quantity. Artisans may need assistance in arriving at a reasonable starting price.

### Buyer Discovery

Artisans may not have an efficient way to identify buyers whose requirements match their products.

### Business Management

After finding customers, artisans also need basic capabilities for managing products, orders and sales information.



## 3. Problem Definition

The core problem addressed by CraftMitra is:

> How can the digital effort required for artisans to create, present, price and market their handmade products be reduced through a simple mobile application?



## 4. Target Users

### Primary Users

- Individual artisans
- Traditional craft producers
- Marginalized artisan communities

### Direct Beneficiaries

- Self-help groups
- Artisan cooperatives
- Small craft businesses

### Secondary Beneficiaries

- Craft retailers
- Government-supported craft networks
- Traditional product buyers



## 5. Proposed Direction

CraftMitra addresses the problem by combining product photography, voice-based input, artificial intelligence-assisted catalog creation, explainable pricing, buyer matching and basic business management into a single mobile workflow.

The intended workflow is:

SHOW → SPEAK → CREATE → PRICE → MATCH → SELL

The system is designed so that the artisan can review and edit important information before publishing a product.



## 6. Expected Outcome

The project aims to reduce the digital effort required to take a traditional craft from the artisan to the digital marketplace.

The application focuses on:

- Simpler product creation
- Voice-based interaction
- Structured digital catalogs
- Transparent price suggestions
- Relevant buyer discovery
- Basic order management
- Sales visibility



## 7. Scope

The current project focuses on the mobile application and its supporting services.

The implementation includes the core product creation, cataloging, pricing, buyer matching, order and sales workflows.

Integration with external marketplace networks, payment systems, logistics providers and production-scale infrastructure is considered future scope unless explicitly implemented in the application.
