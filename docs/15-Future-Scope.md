# 15 — Future Scope

## Overview

CraftMitra is designed as an extensible platform that can be
expanded beyond the current prototype and core application workflow.

## Planned Enhancements

### Marketplace Integration

Connect CraftMitra with suitable digital marketplace networks
to provide artisans with additional selling channels.

### Expanded Language Support

Add support for more Indian languages and improve speech recognition
for regional accents and variations.

### Advanced Pricing Assistance

Future versions can use larger market datasets, regional pricing
trends and historical sales information for improved price assistance.

### Expanded Buyer Network

Increase the number of buyer profiles and connect with larger
business and marketplace networks.

### Analytics

Introduce deeper sales analytics and business insights to help
artisans understand product performance and market activity.

### Production Security

Strengthen cloud security, access controls, image upload security
and application protection mechanisms before production deployment.

## Future Vision

Current Application
        ↓
More Languages
        ↓
Larger Buyer Network
        ↓
Marketplace Integrations
        ↓
Advanced Analytics
        ↓
Wider Digital Market Access

Long-Term Goal

To provide artisans with a simple digital business platform that
helps them create, present, price and sell their traditional
products while reducing technology and digital-market barriers.
